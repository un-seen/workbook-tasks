# Are these companies' HQ cities and CEOs still right as of 12 November 2026?

A ten-company reference table whose rows were all last checked on 30 June 2025. Every row has been re-checked against sources read on 12–13 November 2026: HQ city against the address of principal executive offices on the cover of the company's most recent Form 10-K, and the CEO against a company-controlled page — a leadership listing, a biography, or the press release announcing the appointment.

## The checked table

Two rows had values that were no longer correct, and both were CEO cells. Apple's was Tim Cook; the board announced on 20 April 2026 that John Ternus would become CEO on 1 September 2026,[^aaplceo] and Apple's own leadership page now lists Ternus as CEO.[^appleaders] Disney's was Bob Iger; Josh D'Amaro succeeded him as CEO at the annual meeting on 18 March 2026.[^disceo]

No company in the table changed its HQ city in that period, so no hq city cell was rewritten. Cupertino is still Apple's headquarters[^aaplhq] and Burbank still Disney's[^dishq] — both confirmed on the same annual reports whose CEO checks are cited here.

The other eight rows are unchanged — same HQ city, same CEO — and their "last checked" dates stay at 2025-06-30 as they were. Each was re-verified anyway, and the sources are in the row-by-row record below the table.

## The eight rows that did not change

Each of these was confirmed where it stood, and the confirming source is given per row.

- **Meta Platforms — Menlo Park, CA; Mark Zuckerberg.** The 10-K cover gives the principal executive offices as Menlo Park, California 94025;[^metahq] the same filing describes "Zuckerberg, our founder, Chairman, and CEO".[^metaceo]
- **Tesla — Austin, TX; Elon Musk.** The 10-K cover gives Austin, Texas, and Tesla's Investor Relations address is 1 Tesla Road, Austin, TX 78725;[^tslahq] the filing names "Musk, Technoking of Tesla and our Chief Executive Officer".[^tslaceo]
- **Costco — Issaquah, WA; Ron Vachris.** The 10-K cover gives Issaquah, WA 98027;[^costhq] Costco's investor site lists Ron Vachris as President, CEO and Director since January 2024.[^costceo]
- **Salesforce — San Francisco, CA; Marc Benioff.** The 10-K cover gives San Francisco, California 94105;[^crmhq] the Salesforce leadership page lists Marc Benioff as Chair, CEO & Co-Founder.[^crmceo]
- **Johnson & Johnson — New Brunswick, NJ; Joaquin Duato.** The 10-K cover gives New Brunswick, New Jersey 08933;[^jnfhq] J&J's leadership page names Joaquin Duato Chairman and Chief Executive Officer.[^jnfceo]
- **Bank of America — Charlotte, NC; Brian Moynihan.** The 10-K cover gives Charlotte, North Carolina 28255;[^bachq] the company newsroom biography is of Brian Moynihan as chief executive.[^bacceo]
- **Merck & Co. — Rahway, NJ; Robert Davis.** The 10-K cover gives Rahway, New Jersey 07065, and Merck's fact sheet names Rahway as headquarters;[^mrkhq] Merck's leadership page says "Rob Davis is chairman of the board and chief executive officer of Merck",[^mrkceo] the same man the row records as Robert Davis — so the cell stands as written, with the shorter form of his name the company uses.
- **Lockheed Martin — Bethesda, MD; Jim Taiclet.** The 10-K cover gives Bethesda, Maryland 20817, and Lockheed Martin gives its headquarters as 6801 Rockledge Drive, Bethesda, MD 20817;[^lmthq] its executive leadership page, dated October 2026, names Jim Taiclet Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer.[^lmtceo]
