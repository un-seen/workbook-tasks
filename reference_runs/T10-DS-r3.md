# Headquarters city and chief executive officer of fifteen large US companies

The table below gives, for each of fifteen companies, the city and US state of its corporate headquarters **as printed on the cover page of its latest annual report on Form 10-K**, and the person serving as chief executive officer on 12 November 2026. The cover page is a filing's own statement of its principal executive offices, so each city is cited to the filing it is taken from; the chief executive is taken from the same filing's officer biographies and signature pages. Where a company changed chief executive before or on 12 November 2026, the successor is given instead, cited to whatever source records the change — Berkshire Hathaway and Target in their own filings, Coca-Cola and NVIDIA to company announcements outside them.

## Where the filing and the date disagree

Four companies needed more than their own filing — one of them changed chief executive on the very day asked about. Each is set out below.

A filing describes the company on its filing date, not on 12 November 2026, so each name in the table was also spot-checked against reporting from the months just before that date. Satya Nadella, Sundar Pichai, Andy Jassy, Lip-Bu Tan, Shailesh Jejurikar, Elliott Hill, Brian Niccol, Jamie Dimon and John Furner were all still described as chief executive in coverage from September to November 2026.[^checks] No source found in that check named a successor for any of them.

**NVIDIA — the change falls on the day itself.** The 10-K gives Jen-Hsun Huang as president and chief executive, in office since the company's founding in 1993.[^nvda] On 12 November 2026 — the date asked about — the board announced that "Jensen Huang has resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of the Board, effective immediately", and that "Colette Kress, current Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer of NVIDIA, has agreed to serve as Interim Chief Executive Officer" while a permanent successor is sought.[^nvdachg] The interim appointment is effective immediately, so on 12 November 2026 the office is held by Colette Kress, not by Huang. The board's earlier statement of 8 November 2026 had said it had "confidence in Jensen and his leadership team" and was opening an independent investigation into reporting that Huang had been the target of a foreign intelligence effort;[^nvdaboard] the resignation came four days later. A reader should treat this row as unsettled: the board was still searching for a permanent chief executive as of the announcement.

**Berkshire Hathaway.** The 10-K's officer table still shows Warren E. Buffett, aged 95, as "Chairman of the Board of Directors and Chief Executive Officer", with "Since 1970" against his name. The same exhibit states that "Effective January 1, 2026, Mr. Abel became Berkshire's Chief Executive Officer", and earlier in the filing that the board appointed "Mr. Gregory E. Abel to succeed Mr. Warren E. Buffett as Chief Executive Officer effective January 1, 2026".[^brk] Abel is therefore the chief executive on 12 November 2026.

**Target.** The officer biographies give Michael J. Fiddelke as "Chief Executive Officer since February 2026", with the previous officeholder listed as "Executive Chair since February 2026", having been "Chair of the Board and Chief Executive Officer from August 2014 to January 2026".[^tgt]

**Walmart.** The filing is signed "/s/ John R. Furner" as chief executive, and files as an exhibit a "Retirement Agreement by and between the Company and Doug McMillon dated November 13, 2025".[^wmt]

**Coca-Cola.** The executive-officer biographies record that James Quincey, chairman and chief executive since May 2017, "will step down as Chief Executive Officer effective March 31, 2026, on which date he will transition to Executive Chairman".[^ko] The 10-K names no successor, so the row above rests on two further sources: the company's succession announcement of 10 December 2025, which elected "Executive Vice President and Chief Operating Officer Henrique Braun as CEO, effective March 31, 2026",[^kosucc] and the company's leadership page, which gives Braun as chief executive officer and records that he "began his duties as CEO March 31, 2026".[^kobraun]
