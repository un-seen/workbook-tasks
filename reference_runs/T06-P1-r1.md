# What six equipment prices come to in euros, at the ECB rate of Friday 30 October 2026

Six items priced in US dollars, pounds sterling and yen, converted at the European Central Bank's
euro foreign exchange reference rates for Friday 30 October 2026. The ECB quotes each rate as
units of currency per one euro, so the euro price is the quoted price divided by the rate.[^1]
Each euro figure is rounded to two decimals, and the total is the sum of the rounded figures, not
of the unrounded ones.

The rates are the ones the ECB published for that date: 1.1172 US dollars, 0.8338 pounds sterling
and 175.68 yen to the euro.[^1] The same three rates serve every line of a currency, so the
difference between two dollar lines, or two pound lines, is only the price.
