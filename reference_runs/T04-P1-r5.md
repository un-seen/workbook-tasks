# Total revenue and net income for five US technology companies, as reported in their 10-K

Five companies, each at the fiscal year named in the workbook: total revenue (total net sales) and net income, in USD millions, exactly as the consolidated income statement in that year's Form 10-K sets them out, with a link to the filing on SEC EDGAR. Every figure below is taken from the statement of operations/income in Item 8 (or, for NVIDIA, the financial statements filed under Item 15 of the same 10-K), not from MD&A commentary or a summary table.[^1]

Two names need a word, because the filings do not use the same labels: Apple and Amazon state **total net sales**, Microsoft, NVIDIA and Alphabet state **total revenue** or **revenues** — same line, same meaning.[^2] Alphabet's revenue line is "Revenues" on its consolidated statement of income.[^3]

### What each statement shows

Apple's consolidated statement of operations gives total net sales of $416,161 million, split $307,003 million products and $109,158 million services, and net income of $112,010 million for the year ended 27 September 2025.[^4]

Microsoft's income statement gives total revenue of $281,724 million — $63,946 million product and $217,778 million service and other — and net income of $101,832 million for the year ended 30 June 2025.[^5]

NVIDIA's consolidated statement of income gives revenue of $130,497 million and net income of $72,880 million for the year ended 26 January 2025.[^6]

Alphabet's consolidated statement of income gives revenues of $350,018 million and net income of $100,118 million for the year ended 31 December 2024.[^3]

Amazon's consolidated statement of operations gives total net sales of $637,959 million — $272,311 million product and $365,648 million service — and net income of $59,248 million for the year ended 31 December 2024.[^7]

### Notes on the filings

Each of the five filings is an annual report on Form 10-K whose report period is the fiscal year named in its row, and each was checked to be the filing covering that year rather than a later one: Apple and Microsoft have since filed 10-Ks for fiscal 2026, so for those two the prior year's filing is the one used here.[^8] Each filing's auditor's report covers the same statement the figures come from.[^4]
